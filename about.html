About Us

Our History

Established in 2014 in Hyderabad, Telangana, BAC Infratech Private Limited swiftly emerged as a pioneer in construction and infrastructure development across India. Boasting a formidable team comprising architects, civil, structural, mechanical, electrical engineers, and quantity surveyors, our hallmark lies in our distinctive blend of expertise, precision, and creativity. Over the past decade, our exponential growth has been driven by an unwavering commitment to craftsmanship and client satisfaction. Renowned for our quality, assurance, and engineering proficiency, our portfolio boasts a myriad of lucrative projects, inspiring confidence among clients and partners alike. Our journey is defined by a steadfast commitment to excellence, as evidenced by the successful completion of numerous projects that have transformed visions into reality.

About Us

BAC Infratech Private Limited stands as a seasoned and reliable partner in transforming visions into reality. Embarking on a comprehensive irrigation project, our team specializes in the planning, execution, and maintenance of pipeline installations for efficient and sustainable water distribution in agricultural backdrop. We specialize in installing water supply pipelines across diverse locations, ensuring efficient water distribution and accessibility.

With a decade of experience, we are renowned for quality, reliability, and timely service. Our expertise extends to infrastructure projects, waterways, water mains and lines, irrigation projects, sewage pipelines and other waterworks. Specializing in general construction services, including waterways, irrigation projects, sewage pipelines, and civil engineering works, we have played a pivotal role in providing clean and reliable water sources to villages nationwide.
