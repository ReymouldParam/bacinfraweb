Water Supply Schemes

Water supply schemes refer to organized plans and systems put in place to provide clean, safe, and reliable water to communities, households, or industries. These schemes are essential for ensuring access to potable water, which is fundamental for public health, sanitation, agriculture, and industrial processes. We specialize in designing and implementing customized water supply systems tailored to the specific requirements of each project. Whether it's a small rural community, a sprawling urban area, or an industrial complex, our team of experts develops innovative solutions to meet the demand for water sustainably.

Water Treatment Plants

Water treatment plants are facilities designed to remove contaminants and impurities from raw water to produce safe and clean drinking water or water suitable for various industrial or agricultural uses. These plants are essential for ensuring public health, sanitation, and environmental protection. Our water treatment plants are equipped with state-of-the-art technologies designed to effectively remove contaminants, impurities, and pathogens from water sources. We utilize a combination of physical, chemical, and biological processes, including sedimentation, filtration, disinfection, and advanced oxidation, to produce water that meets or exceeds regulatory standards for potable water. At BAC Infratech, we understand that every water source is unique, with its own set of challenges and characteristics. That's why we offer customized treatment solutions tailored to the specific requirements of each project. Whether it's treating surface water, groundwater, brackish water, or wastewater, our team of experts develops cost-effective and efficient treatment processes that address the specific contaminants and water quality parameters present.

Lift Irrigation Scheme

A lift irrigation scheme is a method used to supply water for agricultural purposes in areas where natural water sources, such as rivers or lakes, are not easily accessible or where the terrain is not conducive to traditional gravity-based irrigation methods. In a lift irrigation scheme, water is lifted from a water source, such as a river or reservoir, to higher elevations using pumps or other mechanical means, and then distributed to agricultural fields through a network of canals, pipelines, or channels. We understand that every agricultural landscape is unique, with its own topographical challenges and water requirements. That's why we offer customized lift irrigation solutions tailored to the specific needs of each location. Whether it's hilly terrain, remote areas, or uneven landscapes, our team of experts designs and implements schemes that maximize water efficiency and minimize energy consumption. Our lift irrigation schemes utilize advanced water lifting technologies such as pumps, turbines, and hydraulic systems to elevate water from lower to higher elevations. We select the most suitable lifting technology based on factors such as water source characteristics, elevation differences, flow rates, and energy availability, ensuring efficient and cost-effective water distribution.
